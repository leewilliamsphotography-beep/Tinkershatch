How to play

Selecting words: Click and drag across letters, or tap the first letter then tap the last letter of a word. Words can run in any of eight directions — including backwards.

Keyboard: Tab into the grid, then use the arrow keys to move between letters. Press Enter to start a selection, extend it with the arrow keys, then press Enter again to confirm. Esc cancels a selection.

Word themes: Words come from East Sussex places, broader British culture, garden flowers, and everyday British things.

Difficulty: Gentle offers a 12×12 grid with 8 words. Classic is 15×15 with 12 words. Hardy is 18×18 with 16 words.

Appearance & sound: Use the Light / Auto / Dark buttons to change the colour theme. Background music is generated live in your browser and is off by default — toggle it with the Music button.
