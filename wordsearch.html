How to play

Selecting words: Click and drag across letters, or tap the first letter then tap the last letter of the word. Words can run in any of eight directions — including backwards.

Keyboard: Tab to move between cells. Press Enter to start a selection, use arrow keys to extend in a straight line, then press Enter again to confirm.

Themes: Words come from East Sussex places, broader British culture, garden flowers, and everyday British things.

Difficulty: Gentle offers a 12×12 grid with 8 words. Classic is 15×15 with 12 words. Hardy is 18×18 with 16 words.
