How to play

Selecting words: Click and drag across letters, or tap the first letter then tap the last letter of a word. Words run in any of eight directions, including backwards.

Keyboard: Tab into the grid, move with the arrow keys. Press Enter to start a selection, extend it with the arrows, press Enter again to confirm. Esc cancels.

Daily puzzle: Turn on the Daily pill — everyone gets the same grid for that day and difficulty, and your result can be shared with the Share button.

Custom lists: Open "Custom word list" above to play with your own words.

Display & sound: Themes (including a high-contrast mode) are in the header, along with large text, generative background music and sound effects. Everything is off or subtle by default.

Shortcuts: N new puzzle · H hint · M music · T theme · Esc cancel selection.

Printing: Use the Print button for a clean paper version; tick "with answers" to include the answer key.
